## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the intellectual gravity, forward-looking ethos, and natural serenity of a premier African international academy. The brand personality balances classical collegiate prestige with the vibrant, cosmopolitan sophistication of contemporary Nairobi. It communicates academic rigor, pastoral warmth, ecological stewardship, and visionary leadership without resorting to colonial clichés or dry institutional tropes.

The visual style is **Contemporary Editorial Luxury with Warm Minimalism**. It adopts generous architectural whitespace, asymmetrical editorial pacing, high-contrast serif headlines, tactile parchment-like foundations, and hairline borders reminiscent of bespoke academic publications. Visual artifacts lean heavily into tactile richness: bespoke photography showcasing sunlight over lush indigenous grounds, authentic student engagement, modern architectural pavilions, and timeless scholarly pursuit.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the highland flora of Kenya and archival bookbinding traditions:

- **Primary (`#133324` / Deep Forest Green):** The institutional anchor. Represents heritage, scholarly depth, and the lush canopy of the campus grounds. Used for dominant headings, prominent call-to-action surfaces, and structural footers. Accompanied by `#0B2016` (Nocturne Forest) for extreme contrast and deep background fields.
- **Secondary (`#C29B38` / Warm Burnished Brass):** Applied with restraint for insignia highlights, active selection indicators, prestigious credentials, and delicate rule accents. Accompanied by `#D4AF37` for luminous micro-interactions.
- **Tertiary (`#EAE3D2` / Soft Sand):** The tonal bridge. Used for structural dividers, key metric panels, secondary card containers, and quiet metadata enclosures.
- **Neutral (`#1C2420` / Highland Charcoal):** Deep slate-charcoal with an earthy undertone. Ensures accessible, legible body typography without the starkness of pure synthetic black.
- **Canvas (`#FBF9F5` / Refined Cream) & Sub-Surface (`#F4EFE6` / Warm Alabaster):** Replaces clinical white with a tactile, editorial paper tone that softens eye strain and reinforces an atmosphere of unhurried prestige.

## Typography

The typographical framework pairs the literary gravitas of **Newsreader** with the geometric precision and openness of **Manrope**.

- **Editorial Serifs (Newsreader):** Handled like bespoke letterpress typography. Use italicized variations for programmatic distinction, pull quotes, and sub-narratives. Optical sizing rules favor high ascenders and relaxed baseline rhythm.
- **Modern Sans (Manrope):** Handles navigational structures, forms, dashboards, and extended reading copy. Its geometric clarity prevents fatigue and provides functional counterbalance to the serif headers.
- **Label & Microcopy Protocol:** All super-titles, category tags, and system markers use uppercase tracked labels (`label-caps`) to invoke formal publication stamps.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an **asymmetric 12-column fluid grid** system framed by disciplined margins. It emphasizes rhythmic pauses and breathing room:

- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Mobile:** `< 640px` (4-column grid, 16px gutters, 20px outer margin)
  - **Tablet:** `640px - 1024px` (8-column grid, 24px gutters, 40px outer margin)
  - **Desktop:** `> 1024px` (12-column grid, 40px gutters, max-width 1360px centered with dynamic page margins)

- **Editorial Rhythm:**
  - Standard marketing sections alternate between full-bleed imagery, offset column compositions (e.g., a 4-column narrative alongside an 8-column triptych gallery), and centered reading columns (columns 3 through 10).
  - Vertical spacing uses an 8px baseline grid. Section breaks employ generous steps (`space-4xl` to `space-5xl`) to create an open gallery ambience rather than a dense web portal.

## Elevation & Depth

To avoid plastic modern gradients and generic SaaS styling, this design system avoids heavy drop shadows. Depth is communicated primarily through **tonal layering and low-contrast perimeter rules**:

- **Paper Layering:** Surfaces exist on stepped tonal planes: Base `#FBF9F5` (Canvas) supports `#F4EFE6` (Contained Panels) and `#FFFFFF` (High-priority interactive floating modules).
- **Hairline Outlines:** Containers, modals, and list items rely on 1px solid borders in `#EAE3D2` or with a subtle translucent wash (`rgba(19, 51, 36, 0.08)`).
- **Ambient Academic Shadow:** When floating elements require separation (e.g., contextual navigation, flyout menus, modals), use an ultra-diffused, tinted shadow:
  - `box-shadow: 0 16px 36px -12px rgba(11, 32, 22, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -4px rgba(11, 32, 22, 0.03);`
- **Atmospheric Dark Fields:** Dedicated signature areas (e.g., Global Footer, Dean’s Portal, Admissions Dossier) invert into deep Forest Green (`#133324` or `#0B2016`) with hairline borders rendered in `#C29B38` at 20% opacity.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a **Soft Architectural (`roundedness: 1`)** geometric language. Curves are subtle and deliberate, echoing tailored stonework and academic book covers rather than hyper-rounded app interfaces:

- **Standard Elements:** Input fields, buttons, small cards, and chips utilize `0.25rem` (4px) radii.
- **Hero Containers & Editorial Vignettes:** Large cards and feature images employ `0.5rem` (8px) radii.
- **Asymmetric Editorial Frame:** Specific image cards feature an architectural variation: top-left and bottom-right corners cut with 16px curves while adjacent corners remain at 4px, giving an asymmetrical framing device reminiscent of modernist East African campus architecture.
- **Pills:** Avoid universal pill shapes except for status indicator dots or circular icon badges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Forest Green (`#133324`) background with Refined Cream (`#FBF9F5`) text. 4px rounded radius. Subtle brass (`#C29B38`) bottom underline effect on hover or a 1px brass border transition.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent surface with a 1px solid border in `#133324` (or `#C29B38` on dark sections), paired with charcoal text and an interactive hover fill transitioning to `#F4EFE6`.
- **Tertiary / Text Link:** Newsreader Italic or tracked Manrope label with a persistent hairline underline positioned 4px beneath the text, shifting in color from `#1C2420` to `#C29B38`.

### Input Fields & Selects
- Background of `#FFFFFF` or `#FBF9F5` set against a `#EAE3D2` border.
- Floating or uppercase tracked labels (`label-caps`) in `#1C2420` with 60% opacity.
- Focus state: Border smoothly shifts to `#133324` with a 2px offset ring in `#C29B38` at 30% opacity.

### Editorial Image & Narrative Cards
- Composed with generous internal padding (`space-xl`).
- High-contrast typography pairing an overarching category tag, an imposing Newsreader title, and an excerpt.
- Photography preserves natural warmth, rich shadows, and natural greenery with minimal post-processing.
- Hover states initiate a delicate 4px image zoom inside an overflow-hidden frame accompanied by an arrow transition.

### Chips & Academic Badges
- Flat, rectangular-soft forms (`rounded: 4px`) carrying uppercase micro-type (`11px`, letter-spacing `0.12em`).
- Tinted background in Soft Sand (`#EAE3D2`) or Warm Alabaster (`#F4EFE6`) with dark Forest Green text.
- Honors or scholarship badges utilize a brass outline (`#C29B38`) with deep green typographic fills.

### Lists & Curricula Modules
- Clean, horizontal row architecture separated by 1px rules in `#EAE3D2`.
- Hover row highlights to `#F4EFE6` with smooth 150ms transitions.
- Index indicators rendered in Newsreader numerals (`01`, `02`, `03`) in `#C29B38` prior to course or module titles.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square checkbox (2px radius) and circular radio. 1.5px solid border in `#133324`.
- Checked state fills with `#133324` featuring a brass (`#C29B38`) or cream check icon.
