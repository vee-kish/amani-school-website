Leadership & Innovation

Cultivating Visionaries for an Evolving Continent

From our flagship Drone & Robotics Guild to youth parliamentary debate, Amani scholars engage real-world technological challenges and community governance while still in school.

Equipped with CNC prototyping benches, Arduino arrays, circuit assembly stations, and 3D printing farms, the guild develops autonomous aerial systems and mechatronic prototypes. Scholars collaborate in teams to solve agricultural drone mapping and wildlife telemetry challenges unique to Kenya.
