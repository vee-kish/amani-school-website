What is the balance between IB and Cambridge curriculums? expand_more

We offer the Cambridge IGCSE framework during Years 10 and 11, giving students globally recognized foundational depth. In Sixth Form (Years 12–13), students select either the full IB Diploma Programme or Cambridge International A-Levels based on their individual academic aspirations and target universities.

How are students supported during university applications? expand_more

Our dedicated University Guidance Counselling suite initiates one-on-one strategy sessions starting in Year 10. Counselors support SAT/ACT prep, UCAS applications, Common App personal essays, and interview coaching with visiting admissions officers from North America, Europe, and Asia.

What pastoral care and safeguarding systems are in place? expand_more

Every student is assigned a dedicated House Tutor and Pastoral Mentor who oversees wellness and academic progress. We maintain an independent student safeguarding committee, full-time clinical psychologists on campus, and strict adherence to international safeguarding protocols.

Are day and flexible boarding options available? expand_more

Amani offers Day, Weekly Boarding (Monday through Friday), and Full Boarding within our contemporary residential houses in the Karen estate, providing structured evening study halls and full weekend excursion programs.
