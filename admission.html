Frequently Addressed Inquiries

What academic curriculum does Amani International Academy follow? expand_more

We offer a blended international continuum. Early Years and Primary (Ages 2-11) follow the International Baccalaureate Primary Years Programme (IB PYP). Middle and Senior secondary students complete the Cambridge IGCSE, culminating in the globally acclaimed International Baccalaureate Diploma Programme (IBDP) in the Sixth Form.

Do you accept mid-year admissions or transfer students from outside Kenya? expand_more

Yes. Given our diplomatic and cosmopolitan parent body, we accommodate mid-year transfers subject to year-group cohort caps. Our transition liaison works closely with incoming families to harmonize educational credits and language placements.

What boarding models are available on the Karen Campus? expand_more

We offer both Full Term Residential Boarding and Weekly Boarding (Sunday evening to Friday afternoon). Boarding begins in Year 7 (approx. age 11). Each House is supervised by live-in Houseparents, resident nurses, and academic tutors.

How are foreign language learners and bilingual students supported? expand_more

We celebrate linguistic diversity. We offer specialized English as an Additional Language (EAL) immersion, alongside native and acquired study in Kiswahili, French, Mandarin, Spanish, and German.
